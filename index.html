NEW HERE?

You don’t have to know anyone
to come belong.

Whether you attend church every week, haven’t been in a long time, or are simply looking for good people and meaningful connection—you’re welcome. Come for an activity that sounds comfortable. There’s no registration unless an event specifically says so.

Spokane Singles 31+ serves the Church’s Single Adult 31+ community and is separate from the Young Single Adult 18–35 program.
